Our capstone · Our team

The people behind Type Tiles.

Type Tiles is a capstone project developed by Wanderpets, a five-member Information Technology team. The project combines typing practice with falling-tile gameplay, Gregg stenography exercises, and teacher-hosted classroom matches to support an engaging learning experience.
